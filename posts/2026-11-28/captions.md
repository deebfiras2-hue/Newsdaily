## c3 3-goodnews-guinea-worm (13:00 UTC)

A disease that hit 3.5 million people now has 14 cases. 🎉

Guinea worm infected millions a year in 1986.
In 2023, just 14 human cases were reported.
No vaccine. No cure. Just clean water, filters and health workers.
It could be the second human disease ever eradicated.
