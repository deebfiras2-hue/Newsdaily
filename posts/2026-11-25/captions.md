## c3 3-didyouknow-venus-day (13:00 UTC)

On Venus, one day lasts longer than one year 🪐

Venus orbits the Sun in about 225 Earth days.
It spins once in about 243 Earth days.
It also spins backwards, so the Sun rises in the west.
The surface is about 465°C, hotter than Mercury.
