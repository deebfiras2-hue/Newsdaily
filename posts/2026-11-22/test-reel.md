# Test reel - BepiColombo reaches Mercury

## Script
Eight years, nine flybys, and one thirty-seven minute burn. Europe and Japan's BepiColombo just entered orbit around Mercury. It launched in twenty eighteen. Only one spacecraft had ever orbited Mercury before: NASA's Messenger. Four thrusters had to slow the probe down, or Mercury's weak gravity would let it fly right past. It worked. Two orbiters split apart in December, and science starts in April. What do you most want to know about the planet closest to the Sun? Follow Zoomed Out for the big picture.
