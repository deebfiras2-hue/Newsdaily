## c2 2-explained-senegal-afcon (10:00 UTC)

Senegal have their Africa Cup title back. Here’s the quick version ⚽

Senegal beat Morocco 1-0 in the final after a walk-off protest.
In March, CAF turned it into a 3-0 forfeit win for Morocco.
On Nov 24 the Court of Arbitration for Sport reversed that.
The panel said the referee restarted the match, so the forfeit rules did not apply.
Morocco says it will keep fighting legally.

## c3 3-goodnews-ozone-recovery (13:00 UTC)

The world fixed the hole in the sky. Seriously 🌍

About 99% of banned ozone-depleting substances are now phased out.
The ozone layer is projected to recover around 2040 for most of the world.
Antarctica is expected to follow around 2066.

## c4 4-numbers-world-population (16:00 UTC)

Humanity will stop growing at 10.3 billion.

That is the UN’s medium projection, reached in the mid-2080s.
The world had about 8.2 billion people in 2024.
Fertility fell from 4.9 births per woman in 1950 to 2.25 in 2024.
One in four people live in a country whose population has already peaked.
All figures: UN World Population Prospects 2024.
